---
title: "The Dastardly Cousins"
date: 2026-10-04
---

Two related flavors of flawed thinking:

> [**McNamara fallacy:**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McNamara_fallacy) Making decisions only based on what can be (easily) measured.

and

> [**Goodhart's law:**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodhart%27s_law) Turning a measure into a target, making it useless as either.

You might call these "the dastardly cousins," because they share a common DNA: mistaking the proxy (what you can measure) for the real thing (meaningful outcomes). This comes up in knowledge work all the time: things like innovation, creativity, or deep understanding have so far resisted all attempts at turning them into a KPI to be maximized.

The LLM era has caused many people to become ignorant of this reality. Hence the tokenmaxxing, slop grenades, and overall cargo cult of "more, more, more" with little to show for it.
